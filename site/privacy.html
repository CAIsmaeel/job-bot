Privacy policy

FinRehearse ("we") respects your privacy.

Practice data

Everything you post in the simulator stays in your browser storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

Licence key

Your licence key is checked inside your browser and kept in your browser storage. The app does not contact a server to check it.

Purchase information

When you buy, the payment provider collects your payment details. We receive your name, email and the amount paid so we can send your key and give support. We do not see your card details.

Analytics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track you across websites. It tells us only totals, such as how many people opened a page. We do not use advertising trackers or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the website.

Your rights

Write to finrehearse.help@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.
