Delivery and refund policy

What you buy

A licence key that unlocks the full version of the FinRehearse simulator in your web browser. Nothing physical is shipped.

Delivery

After your payment is confirmed we email your licence key and the app link to the email address you paid with, within 24 hours. If you have not received it after 24 hours, check your spam folder, then write to finrehearse.help@gmail.com.

7-day refund

If you are not happy with the full version, write to finrehearse.help@gmail.com within 7 days of payment from the email you paid with. You do not need to give a reason. We refund the full amount to your original payment method. Refunds are started within 2 working days of your request; your bank may take 5 to 7 working days more to show it.

Cancellation

There is no subscription and no automatic renewal, so there is nothing to cancel. Your access simply ends when the licence period ends.

A refunded key is withdrawn and stops working.
