What you practise

01

Payables and receivables

Vendor and customer invoices, payments, partial payments, residual items, credit memos, duplicate checks and reversals.

02

Procure to pay

Purchase order, goods receipt, invoice verification and the GR/IR clearing account that trips up many candidates.

03

Bank reconciliation

Match a bank statement to the books, find duplicates, post charges and interest, and explain timing differences.

04

Month-end closing

Accruals and their reversal, a closing checklist and period locks. Try to post into a closed period and see why you cannot.

05

Fixed assets and tax

Capitalise, depreciate and retire assets. Change a GST rate from a date and see old and new documents stay correct.

06

Sales to billing, and returns

Sales order, credit limit, delivery with cost of goods sold, billing, customer returns and credit memos.

07

FP&A: budget versus actual

Find the biggest overspend, split a variance into price and volume, and explain the month in one line, the way FP&A interviews ask.

08

Payroll and inventory

Run a monthly payroll with PF, TDS and professional tax, count stock, post shortages and prove the valuation to the ledger.

09

Interview preparation

Fresher basics lessons with check questions, 165 interview questions with model answers and a recall drill, and a one-page revision sheet for the night before.

10

Certificate of completion

A printable FinRehearse record of what you completed. It is our own practice certificate, not an SAP certification.

Built to teach, not to flatter

Outcome-based missions

A mission passes only when the ledger shows the right business result. A technically posted but wrong entry does not pass.

Guided, independent and interview modes

Learn with steps, then with only the business scenario, then under a timer with no menu and no hints. You get a skill report.

It tells you the right answer

When your answer is wrong you are shown the correct one and why.

Questions before you buy

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in Chrome, Safari or any current browser, on phone or laptop. A laptop is easier for long reports.

Can I use one key on my phone and my laptop?

Yes, the key is for you, on any of your devices. Your practice progress is saved in each browser separately, and you can move it with Export and Import.

How do I get the full version after paying?

The key is emailed to the address you paid with, usually within 20 minutes. Tap the link in the email and the full version switches on. If you do not see it, check spam, then write to finrehearse.help@gmail.com.

Do I need to know SAP already?

No. Freshers start with short basics lessons and guided missions. Experienced users can jump to the interview simulation.

Is the certificate accepted by employers?

It is our own practice certificate, not an SAP certification. It shows what you practised, and an employer can check it on our verify page. The real value is that you can explain the work in the interview.

What if I do not like it?

Write to finrehearse.help@gmail.com within 7 days of payment for a full refund. No reason needed.

Does it renew automatically?

No. You pay once. Access ends when your 3 or 12 months end, and you are not charged again.
