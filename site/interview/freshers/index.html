How to prepare for an accounts fresher (B.Com, M.Com) interview

First interviews for accounts roles check basics: debit and credit, GST, a simple journal entry, and whether you understand how an ERP records everyday work. You do not need experience, but you need to show you can learn the system quickly.

What interviewers check

  • Golden rules of debit and credit
  • Journal entries for purchase, sale, payment and receipt
  • Basics of GST and TDS
  • What a ledger, trial balance and balance sheet show
  • How an ERP handles vendors, customers and the general ledger
  • Excel basics: VLOOKUP, pivot tables

Practise it, don't just read it. Interviewers can tell when you have only memorised answers. Post the entries yourself in a practice ERP and you can explain every step.

Common questions, with short answers

You buy goods on credit for Rs. 10,000 plus 18% GST. What is the entry?

Dr Purchases 10,000, Dr Input GST 1,800, Cr Vendor 11,800. The vendor is a liability because you owe the money.

What is the difference between a ledger and a trial balance?

A ledger shows all postings to one account. The trial balance lists the closing balance of every account; total debits must equal total credits.

What is an ERP?

One system where purchasing, sales, inventory and accounts share the same data. When stores post a goods receipt, accounts sees the liability at once without re-entering it.

What is depreciation?

Spreading the cost of a fixed asset over its useful life, because the asset helps earn income for several years.

Why should we hire a fresher like you?

Say what you have practised: for example, that you have posted invoices, payments and a month-end close in a practice ERP and can explain each entry. Concrete practice beats general claims.
