How to prepare for a Record to Report (R2R) and GL accountant interview

R2R interviews test the general ledger and month-end close: journal entries, accruals, reconciliations and a clean trial balance on time.

What interviewers check

  • Journal entries and reversals
  • Accruals, prepayments and provisions
  • Month-end close checklist and period close
  • Balance sheet reconciliations
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Trial balance review and intercompany basics

Practise it, don't just read it. Interviewers can tell when you have only memorised answers. Post the entries yourself in a practice ERP and you can explain every step.

Common questions, with short answers

What is an accrual and when is it reversed?

An accrual records an expense in the month it belongs to when the invoice has not arrived, for example Dr Electricity, Cr Accrued expenses. It is reversed on the first day of the next month so that when the real invoice is posted the cost is not counted twice.

Walk me through a month-end close.

Cut-off for postings, accruals and prepayment releases, depreciation, reconciliations of bank, vendors, customers and GR/IR, intercompany matching, review of the trial balance and variances, then close the period so nothing more is posted.

How do you correct a wrong journal entry?

Reverse it with a reversal document that refers to the original, then post the correct entry. You never edit or delete a posted document because the audit trail must show both.

What is a balance sheet reconciliation?

Proving that a ledger balance is supported by detail, such as a bank statement, a sub-ledger or a schedule. Every difference is explained and old items are cleared.

Why can't you post directly to a reconciliation account?

Accounts like trade payables are updated only from vendor or customer postings so the sub-ledger and the general ledger always agree.
